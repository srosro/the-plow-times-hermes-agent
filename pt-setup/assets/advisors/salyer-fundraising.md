# Fundraising — while raising, at any stage

## Signals
- Actively raising, or planning a raise in the next 1–2 quarters.
- Unsure what ARR or traction maps to which round in today's market.

## Focus first
- Ignore the round name; raise the right amount, at a valuation you can grow into, to hit the milestones that unlock the next stage.
- Know the market: "The Series A is kind of like what the Series B used to be, and the seed is what the A used to be."
- Pitch "why now": acute urgency and a tailwind such as new regulation, a shift in behavior, or newly possible technology.
- Tell tangible customer stories: who the customer is, what they cared about, their alternatives, and why they chose you.
- Show a moat on two sides: product (switching costs, the system of record) and go-to-market (an unfair edge in acquiring customers).
- Bring a pitch deck; it shows the storytelling enterprise founders need and works as a leave-behind.
- Diligence your investors: talk to portfolio founders, including ones who failed, and learn the fund's reserves; a board member is a 5–10-year partner.
- Warm intros convert best, but a well-executed cold email is "Sales 101."

## Do not focus on
- Raising too little at too high a valuation, then missing the next round's bar — his biggest mistake.
- Anchoring on round names or vanity valuations.
- Manufacturing FOMO with arbitrary deadlines, or naming a raise or dilution target with no context.
- Adding "AI" to the deck as window dressing.
- Pitching before you have talked to tens or hundreds of customers.
- Saving all questions for the end of the pitch.

## Benchmarks
- A competitive 2025 Series A needs $3–5M ARR, the old Series B bar; in 2016 ~$1M ARR raised an A and $4–5M a B.
- Top-quartile 2025 pre-money: Series A ~$83M (2015 B: ~$73M), Seed ~$23M (2015 A: ~$24M), Pre-Seed ~$12M (2015 Seed: ~$8.5M).
- Top-decile 2025 pre-money: Series A ~$135M, Seed ~$32M, Pre-Seed ~$17M.
- 95th-percentile round size: the 2025 Series A and the 2015 Series B are both ~$65M.

## Exit criteria
- Enough runway to clear the next stage's milestone bar, not merely to survive, at a valuation you can grow into without a down round.
